About Me

I'm a data-driven marketing professional with an MBA and a passion for finding smarter ways to connect people, ideas, and technology.

My work sits at the intersection of marketing, analytics, and emerging technology. I enjoy using data to understand audiences, optimize campaigns, and turn insights into strategies that actually move the needle.
