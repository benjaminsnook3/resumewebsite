Project Scope

Built and scaled a product-based digital storefront by owning all major functions: product concepting, listing optimization, branding, fulfillment workflows, and customer communication.

Core Responsibilities

  • Brand identity and product presentation
  • Product listing copy and search-focused keywords
  • Order management and fulfillment quality control
  • Customer support and review management

Marketing Strategy

  • Consistent visual storytelling across listings
  • Price and offer testing for conversion improvement
  • Seasonal product planning and launch timing
  • Continuous iteration based on customer feedback

Impact

The project demonstrates practical digital marketing, ecommerce operations, and creative entrepreneurship. It also reflects the ability to combine design thinking with measurable business outcomes in a real-world environment.
